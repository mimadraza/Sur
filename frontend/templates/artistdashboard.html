About The Artist
Hozier, born Andrew John Hozier-Byrne on March 17, 1990, in Bray, County Wicklow, Ireland, is a renowned Irish singer-songwriter and musician, celebrated for his soulful voice and poetic lyrics that blend elements of indie rock, blues, soul, and folk music. His musical style has often been compared to the likes of artists such as Leonard Cohen, Jeff Buckley, and Van Morrison. Hozier gained worldwide fame with his debut single "Take Me to Church" from his self-titled debut album, which was released in 2014. The song quickly became a global hit, reaching No. 1 on multiple international charts and earning Grammy nominations. The song's haunting melodies and socially conscious lyrics about love, faith, and human rights made it an anthem for many.
